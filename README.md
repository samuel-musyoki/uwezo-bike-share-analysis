# uwezo-bike-share-analysis
Uwezo bike shares is a personal project i did out of  curiosity to know the revenue generated by uwezo bikes company,The amount of employees the company has and the sum of profits that the company earns 
Uwezo Bike Share – Data Analysis Project

 Tools & Technologies Used

SQL – for data cleaning, querying, aggregation, and calculating monthly metrics

Power BI – for data visualization, dashboard creation, and performance analysis

 Project Objectives

Determine the total number of registered members per month

Calculate total revenue and total profit generated

Analyze ride trends across months

Evaluate seasonal business performance

 Key Insights

Total profit generated: 15.14 million

Total revenue generated: 15.19 million

Ride activity steadily increased from January to September, then slightly declined

Profit consistently increased from Season 1 to Season 4

The majority of rides were made by registered members

Conclusion

The analysis indicates strong business growth, particularly in profit across seasons. The steady increase in rides during the first three quarters suggests high demand and operational efficiency, while the slight drop after September may reflect seasonal variation.
